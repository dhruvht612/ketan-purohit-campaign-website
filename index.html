Meet the candidate

What do I bring?

Hi, I am Ketan Purohit, and I bring over 30 years of experience working with municipalities and public institutions, giving me a strong understanding of governance, accountability, and community needs.

As a Mechanical Engineering graduate, a devoted husband of 34 years, and the proud father of two successful children, I understand the importance of quality education and strong family values.

For more than 25 years, I have been actively involved in youth programs and volunteer work with non-profit organizations, helping young people reach their full potential.

Having lived and traveled in diverse communities around the world, I value inclusion, respect, and opportunity for all.

As your School Trustee, I am committed to supporting student success, strengthening partnerships with families, and ensuring responsible stewardship of education resources.

Why I am running

Every Child Deserves

  • A safe and respectful learning environment as a fundamental human right.

  • Equal opportunities for success based on their own qualities and strengths.

  • Strong moral and character development to become a better citizen.

  • Support for mental wellbeing, building stronger families and communities.

  • Schools that work together with families and communities for true inclusion.
